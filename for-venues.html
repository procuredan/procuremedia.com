Every venue runs on capacity it can't get back — the empty seat, the unrented gun, the quiet tee time, the open bar stool. We fill that idle capacity with paying customers through UltimatePlayPass, and you keep the high-margin spend once they're inside.

The Math

The real money was never in the ticket.

An empty seat earns nothing — that revenue is gone for good. But a customer in that seat buys food, drinks, rentals, souvenirs, and pro-shop items. Discounting admission to fill capacity isn't lost margin; it's the cheapest customer acquisition you'll ever do.

ACCEPTABLE USE POLICY

Effective Date: July 7, 2026 • Version 1.0

1. Overview

1.1 Purpose. This Acceptable Use Policy (the “AUP”) describes conduct that is prohibited when accessing or using the services of Procure Media, LLC (“Procure”). It is part of, and incorporated into, the Procure Media Service Agreement (the “Agreement”). Capitalized terms not defined here have the meaning given in the Agreement.

1.2 Your Responsibility. You are responsible for your own use of the Services, for your users, and for any activity under your account. Violation of this AUP is a material breach of the Agreement and may result in immediate suspension or termination for cause under Section 6 of the Agreement, without refund, in addition to any other remedy available to Procure.

1.3 Examples Only; Not Exhaustive. This AUP describes examples of prohibited conduct but is not exhaustive. Procure may determine that other conduct is prohibited if it creates legal, operational, security, reputational, or business risk. Nothing in this AUP limits Procure’s right to refuse service to, or to suspend or terminate, any customer or account that Procure determines, in its sole and absolute discretion, presents unacceptable legal, regulatory, financial, operational, security, or reputational risk.

2. Compliance With Law

You will not use the Services in any way that violates any applicable law or regulation. Without limitation, you will not use the Services to violate:

  • The Telephone Consumer Protection Act (TCPA), the Telemarketing Sales Rule (TSR), the CAN-SPAM Act, or any state telemarketing, autodialer, or “do-not-call” law;

  • Any consumer-protection, unfair-or-deceptive-practices, licensing, or advertising law applicable to your business or the products or services you sell;

  • Any privacy or data-protection law applicable to the personal information you receive or process; or

  • Any anti-fraud, anti-spam, intellectual-property, or export-control law.

3. Consent, Contact, and Messaging Rules

3.1 Consent. Before contacting any Lead by call, text, or email, you must obtain and maintain all legally required consents. You may not rely on Procure to have obtained consent on your behalf.

3.2 Do-Not-Call and Opt-Outs. You must maintain your own internal do-not-call list, honor all opt-out and unsubscribe requests promptly, and comply with calling-time restrictions and identification requirements.

3.3 Telephony, SMS, and Carrier Compliance. If you use any Procure calling or messaging feature, you must comply with all applicable carrier requirements, messaging-provider policies (for example, Twilio, Bandwidth, and similar providers), and industry best practices (including CTIA guidelines and A2P 10DLC / toll-free registration and messaging-content rules), maintain records of consent, and not send unlawful robocalls, spam, or prohibited message content.

3.4 No Harassment. You will not use the Services to harass, threaten, defraud, deceive, or abuse any Lead, consumer, or other person.

3.5 Recordkeeping. You must maintain records reasonably sufficient to demonstrate your compliance with applicable consent, marketing, and communications laws, and provide them to Procure on reasonable request.

4. Prohibited Uses of Leads and the Services

You will not:

  • Resell, re-license, share, rent, lease, syndicate, redistribute, copy, publish, post, export, bulk-download, or create databases from Leads, or otherwise make Leads available to any third party;

  • Create, submit, solicit, or transmit fake, fraudulent, duplicate, or test Leads, including submissions made by your employees, agents, affiliates, or competitors, or submissions using fake or misappropriated identities;

  • Use Leads or the Services for any unlawful, deceptive, discriminatory, predatory, or fraudulent purpose, or for identity theft;

  • Use Leads for any purpose other than your own lawful business use as permitted by the Agreement;

  • Make false, misleading, or unsubstantiated claims, or engage in bait-and-switch or other deceptive marketing; or

  • Misrepresent your identity, your affiliation, or the origin of any communication.

  • Counterfeit products

  • Firearms and ammunition

  • Adult entertainment

  • Escort services

  • Gambling

  • Sweepstakes

  • Multi-level marketing (unless approved in writing by Procure)

  • Payday loans

  • Debt settlement

  • Bail bonds

  • Cryptocurrency investment schemes

  • Get-rich-quick offers

  • Miracle or unsubstantiated health claims

  • Fake reviews or testimonials

  • Government impersonation

  • Political fundraising

  • Anything Procure determines, in its sole discretion, creates excessive legal or reputational risk.

6. Platform, Security, and Technical Restrictions

You will not:

  • Attempt to gain unauthorized access to, probe, scan, or test the vulnerability of the Services or any related system or network;

  • Interfere with, disrupt, overload, or degrade the Services, or circumvent any security or authentication measure;

  • Circumvent or attempt to circumvent usage limits, billing systems, Lead allocation, pricing, credit policies, or other technical or account controls;

  • Reverse engineer, decompile, scrape, crawl, or use automated means to extract data from the Platform, including high-volume scraping, automated extraction, credential stuffing, or rate-limit bypass, except to the extent this restriction is prohibited by law;

  • Introduce malware, spam, phishing, or other harmful or unlawful code or content;

  • Share, sell, or transfer your account credentials, or allow unauthorized users to access the Services;

  • Use the Services, Leads, platform outputs, prompts, scripts, workflows, or documentation to train, fine-tune, benchmark, or improve any artificial-intelligence or machine-learning system;

  • Use the Services to build, benchmark, performance-test, or competitively analyze a competing product or service, or to copy Procure’s workflows or methods;

  • Attempt to identify, contact, solicit, or transact directly with Procure’s vendors, carriers, publishers, advertising sources, or other partners in order to circumvent Procure, or reverse engineer Procure’s lead sources or relationships;

  • Use the Services in a manner that could reasonably harm Procure’s reputation or its relationships with vendors, carriers, publishers, advertisers, regulators, payment processors, or business partners; or

  • Engage in any activity that causes Procure to violate, or risk violating, the policies of payment processors, telecommunications providers, cloud providers, advertising platforms, or other service providers.

7. Data Protection and Security

7.1 Consumer Data. You will handle all personal information you receive through the Services in accordance with applicable law, keep it secure, use it only for the lawful purpose for which it was provided, and honor any access, deletion, or opt-out request you are required to fulfill.

7.2 No Enrichment for Prohibited Purposes. You will not combine, append, or enrich Lead data to build profiles for unlawful, discriminatory, or prohibited uses.

7.3 Data Retention and Destruction. You will securely destroy Lead and consumer data when it is no longer needed for the purpose for which it was provided or when required by law.

7.4 Security Incident Reporting. You will promptly notify Procure of any unauthorized access, security incident, or compromise involving Procure data or Leads.

8. Enforcement

8.1 Sole Discretion; No Duty to Monitor. Procure has the sole and absolute discretion to determine whether conduct violates this AUP, and Procure’s determination is final, subject to applicable law. Procure has no obligation to monitor use of the Services, but may do so.

8.2 Suspension and Termination. Procure may suspend, restrict, disable, or permanently terminate access, remove or disable content, throttle usage, or take any other action it deems appropriate, immediately and without prior notice, and without liability or refund, for any actual or suspected violation of this AUP or the Agreement, or where a customer’s activities expose Procure to legal, regulatory, operational, security, carrier, payment-processor, advertiser, or reputational risk. Procure may report suspected unlawful activity to law enforcement and cooperate with authorities.

8.3 Documentation and Audit. Procure may request reasonable documentation demonstrating your compliance with this AUP and applicable law, and you agree to provide it within a reasonable time.

8.4 No Refund. Suspension or termination for a violation of this AUP is a termination for cause; Fees already paid are non-refundable and the end-of-period continuation described in the Agreement does not apply.

8.5 Reporting Abuse. To report a suspected violation of this AUP, contact Procure at dan@procuremedia.com.

9. Changes to This AUP

Procure may update this AUP from time to time. Procure will post the updated AUP and revise the “Last Updated” date. Your continued use of the Services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated AUP.

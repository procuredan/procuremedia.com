What We Do

Two engines. One performance marketing company.

We build and operate our own websites, drive high-intent traffic to them, and turn that traffic into customers — either as qualified leads for buyers, or as paying customers for experiences.

Engine 01

Lead Generation

Most lead and list brokers sell the same lead several times — so agents end up working a mix of real-time and aged records that three other people already called. Procure works the opposite way. We build a site around your exact buy — your fields, your qualifiers, your geography — and the leads it generates go to you, and only you.

Your fields. Your qualifiers. Your leads.

They cost more than broker lists — and here's why they're worth it:

  • ✓
    Exclusive, never resold. Generated from our own traffic and delivered to a single buyer — you.
  • ✓
    Higher contact rates. Real people completing real forms with real intent, not recycled records.
  • ✓
    Higher close rates. Sourced from genuine search traffic, at the moment of need.
Engine 02

Customer Acquisition

Every venue runs on capacity it can't get back. An empty seat at the game, an unrented paintball gun, a quiet weekday tee time, a half-full dining room — that revenue is gone for good. And the real money was never in the ticket. It's in what customers spend once they're inside.

Empty seats don't spend — customers do.

We turn idle capacity into on-site revenue:

  • ✓
    Admission is just the hook. Customers buy deeply discounted passes on UltimatePlayPass for seats that were sitting empty anyway.
  • ✓
    You keep the high-margin spend. Food, drinks, souvenirs, paint and CO2, cart and gear rentals, pro-shop items, concessions — the real profit walks in the door.
  • ✓
    Built for any ticketed experience. Rafting, golf, and paintball; pro and semi-pro sports; concerts, rodeos, and races; bars and restaurants.
